## Remaining launch acceptance

Real ChatGPT and Claude connections have not yet been tested. Complete their product connection checks, a reviewed GitHub correction and subsequent retrieval before advertising a production endpoint. SDK success is not product acceptance. Keep production website connection variables unset until that decision.

The deployment preflight is not a permanent billing lock. Keep the account on Workers Free without paid integrations; an administrator can change account plans. Free quota exhaustion can deny service. No usage alert or application rate limiter substitutes for the Free provider cutoff.
