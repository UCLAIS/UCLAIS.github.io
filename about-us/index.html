About Us

A student community for learning, building and researching AI, safely and ethically.

Join us

A society for everyone interested in AI.

UCL AI Society brings together students who want to understand AI, use it thoughtfully and help shape its future.

We want AI to be useful to people. That means asking what a system can do, where it can fail and who is affected by the way it is built and used.

Our community connects technical work with questions about safety, fairness, privacy and accountability. You can learn the foundations, join a research team or build with others, while thinking critically about the consequences.

Our goals

Make AI accessible

Give students from different backgrounds a way to understand AI and develop practical skills.

Support student research

Help students find collaborators, explore questions and develop work they can share with the wider research community.

Open up opportunities

Connect students with researchers, companies and each other through projects, events and career-building partnerships.

Safe and ethical AI

The eight principles we stand by.

  1. Never Build for Lethality
  2. Never Use AI to Impersonate a Human
  3. Defend Human Creativity
  4. Welcome Every Student
  5. Ask Anything, Help Anyone
  6. Build, Don’t Regurgitate
  7. Question the Hype
  8. No AI Slop

Connections beyond the classroom.

Our partnerships give students practical experience and career support.
