From a question to a project.

Nexus Labs is the research division of UCL AI Society. We help students turn shared research interests into work they can present.

Find your team

Tell us what interests you. We group applicants by research interests and help teams form.

Develop the work

Share progress through demos and poster sessions, with feedback from peers and researchers.

Share the results

Present to students and society partners, with support for strong projects working towards conference submissions.

Four research pillars.

Choose the questions you want to work on.

Computational Foundations

The theory and algorithms behind AI: learning, optimisation, probabilistic methods and reasoning.

Explore this pillar
Embodied Intelligence

AI that interacts with the physical world: robot learning, perception, control and vision-language-action models.

Explore this pillar
Human-centred AI

How AI works with people: human-AI interaction, explainability, safety, multimodal perception and evaluation.

Explore this pillar
AI for Healthcare

Applying AI to biology and medicine, from medical imaging and genomics to drug discovery and clinical decision support.

Explore this pillar

Two ways to research.

Both tracks include Nexus events and opportunities to showcase your work.

Advanced

For students with solid technical foundations who are ready to commit consistent time to ambitious research.

Closer mentorship and support from academic and industry researchers. Continued participation depends on progress and engagement.

Exploratory

For students discovering research, developing their skills or trying out early ideas.

A more flexible environment for experimentation and learning. Members may move here from the Advanced track if it becomes a better fit.
