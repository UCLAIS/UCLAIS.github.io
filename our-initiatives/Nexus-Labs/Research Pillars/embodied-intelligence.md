# 🤖 Embodied Intelligence  
### *Where physical interaction becomes intelligence.*

**Embodied Intelligence** studies how intelligence emerges from interacting with the physical world, blending robotics, perception, control, and agentic reasoning.

---

## 🔍 Research Areas

- Robot learning and control  
- Multi-agent systems  
- Human–robot interaction 
- Vision-language-action models  
- Sim2Real transfer  
- Embodied cognition  

---

## 🧩 What We Explore

Projects span robotic locomotion, manipulation, reinforcement learning for control, and real-world deployment of embodied agents.
