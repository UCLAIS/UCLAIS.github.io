# When will I hear about subcommittee interviews and decisions?

Subcommittee interviews and decisions are rolling. Applicants should expect further communication as applications are reviewed.

This is the society's recruitment update as of 2 November 2026. It does not confirm the outcome of an individual application or specify a deadline for all subcommittee decisions.
