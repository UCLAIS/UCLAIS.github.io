# When will I receive my Nexus Labs application decision?

Nexus Labs applications are closed. Decisions are being released gradually. Applicants should expect their decision by the end of the week commencing 2 November 2026.

This is the society's recruitment update as of 2 November 2026. It does not confirm the outcome of an individual application.
