# How do I join UCL AI Society, and how much does membership cost?

Standard membership costs £5. Join through the [official Students’ Union UCL membership page](https://studentsunionucl.org/clubs-societies/artificial-intelligence-society). You need to log in to the Union website to join.

The Union also lists a free taster membership. Check the Union page for the current membership options and price. For questions the published information does not answer, email info@uclai.co.uk.

The public UCL AI Society MCP is read-only and open to everyone; no society account is required to use it.
