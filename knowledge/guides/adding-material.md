# How to add Society knowledge

Maintainers can add tutorials, Nexus session notes, FAQs and guides to the `knowledge/` folder in the UCL AI Society website repository. Review the facts, attribution and permission to publish before merging the change. Once the deployment workflow is configured, merging to `main` builds and publishes the updated public knowledge MCP.

Markdown files need a heading and their content. Optional front matter can give a title, topic, academic year and archive status. Notebooks are imported as ordered cells without execution outputs. PDF text is imported with page references. Linked videos and slide decks remain links unless an authorised body or transcript has been imported.

Public GitHub branches are public even when a file is marked as a draft for the MCP. Keep confidential committee material and student personal information out of the public repository.

For missing information, uncertain policies, corrections or questions outside the library, email info@uclai.co.uk. AI answers may be incorrect; check their cited sources and dates.
