# Tutorials and learning materials

Explore UCL AI Society tutorial notes, notebooks and resources for self-study at https://uclai.co.uk/our-initiatives/tutorials/.

Published files from `knowledge/tutorials/` automatically create entries and reading pages after a reviewed merge and successful website deployment. The same files are available in the public MCP after its deployment. Check the actual published entries for the materials available in a given academic year.
